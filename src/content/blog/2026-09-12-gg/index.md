Yesterday was a sunny, beautiful day. It was also my last day at Visma
Solutions. My journey at Visma was great. I walked in as a summer trainee in
2024 and walked out as a software developer, learning to build professional
software alongside some extraordinary people along the way. I even managed to
graduate twice during my time there. 

The best part of Visma was the people, whom I even spent some free time with in
the form of birthday parties and videogames (Visma's CS2 and Rocket League
teams!). Trips to the sunny Lappeenranta were always nice even though I never
tasted *hydrogen* or *atom*. 

A massive thanks to everyone at Visma who took a chance on me, reviewed my code,
coached me, shared lunch, or carried me in a match. On to the next adventure.
